Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Balsamic Vinegar
  • Honey
  • Garlic
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Fresh Gluten Free Fettuccine
  • Butter
  • Parmesan

Steps

  1. Put Chicken, Balsamic Vinegar, Honey, Garlic, and Salt & Pepper in a bowl and let it sit for 30 Minutes
  2. Cook Chicken in olive oil in a skillet with some of the sauce
  3. In a sauce pan put balsamic vinegar, honey, garlic, and Salt & Pepper. Bring to a boil and simmer for 10 min
  4. Cook pasta and toss in b=utter, garlic, parmesan, and Salt & pepper.
  5. Pour sauce over cooked chicken.
